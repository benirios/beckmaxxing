## Evidence scope

Sources include peer-reviewed papers, author manuscripts, publisher abstracts,
preprints, original public strategy tests, and official market/contract
methodologies. Source type is stated where material. Several publisher
full-text links were inaccessible; only retrievable abstracts, author copies
or official descriptions support their summarized findings. A link is not an
independent replication. In particular, T056 uses daily source data; T031,
T032, T033, T040, T049, T050, T067, T084, T085, T087, T089 and T090 make
substantial horizon or asset transfers. T038/T039 rely on an unaudited
preprint simulation, and T074/T079/T080 include original public tests rather
than peer-reviewed efficacy evidence. T078 remains unsuitable for the
vault's FTMO path. T072/T081/T087 require proprietary position/order data.

Every entry names a falsifiable intraday prediction or policy comparison;
its source need not assert that prediction. Shared cost, publication-time,
control, untouched-date and account-equity gates are at the top of the
catalog. A fully frozen implementation specification is a later research
step, not part of this collection task.
